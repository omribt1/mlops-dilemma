        Where to send prompts over 32K

        Check the hourly answer request by request

        The hourly model works with averages. The simulation replays a week one request at a time: requests arrive at random, some short and some very long, each GPU works through its queue in arrival order, and anything that would wait goes to the managed API up to its rate limit. A fleet passes only if no more than 1 in 100 short and 1 in 100 long requests finish later than your latency budget.

        It shows what averages hide (surges inside an hour, a few huge prompts holding up a GPU, work piling up over days) and tests the alternatives: routing long prompts to the API, spreading them out, or running them at night.

          Measure it on Agent Platform

          Deploys one replica of the selected model and hardware to an Agent Platform endpoint, runs a concurrency sweep with your prompts, and replaces the estimate with measured throughput.
